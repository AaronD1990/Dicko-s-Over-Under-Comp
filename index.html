For a finished season the final win total is used. During a season, projected wins is the team's win percentage times 82, so the board moves as the standings change.

Early in a season a few games swing the projections a lot, so the board will jump around until the standings settle. A team with no games played yet counts as no pick for everyone.

Each correct pick is 1 point, 30 available. Picks marked $ are sure things: the bonus is the share you got right, out of the number you marked, scaled to 5 points. Total is out of 35.
